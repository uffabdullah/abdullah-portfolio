The problem

Manual attendance codes and self-reported location can be spoofed. ProxyProof uses physical presence as a trust signal: rotating Bluetooth tokens, approved-device binding, room geolocation, and a live organiser-controlled session.

01 / PRESENCE

Rotating five-second Bluetooth signals

02 / TRUST

Student devices are bound and reviewed

03 / REPORTING

Live rosters, attendance history, and CSV export

How it works

01

Create a session

The organiser starts a timed session while keeping the Android beacon active.

02

Verify presence

Students join by approved device and confirm the rotating token with their classroom location.

03

Review attendance

The organiser watches the roster update and exports verified attendance records.
