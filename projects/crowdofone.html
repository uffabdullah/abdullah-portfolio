RoleProduct & AI Prototype
StackReact · TypeScript · Three.js
StatusWorking prototype

The problem

Early product feedback is often limited, biased, or expensive. CrowdOfOne creates a synthetic audience made from varied LLM personas that can react consistently to an idea before real user research begins.

01 / INPUT

Paste a pitch, app description, or landing page

02 / SIMULATION

Generate varied audience personas and objections

03 / OUTPUT

Turn scattered reactions into one actionable report

The approach

01

Define personas

Build a diverse set of simulated audience profiles with different goals and priorities.

02

Run reactions

Measure confusion, objections, and willingness to pay across the simulated audience.

03

Surface patterns

Aggregate recurring feedback into a report that a founder can review quickly.
