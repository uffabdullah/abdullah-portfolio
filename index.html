I’m Abdullah Asger Ali, an 18-year-old B.Tech. Computer Science and Engineering student specialising in Data Science at VIT Vellore, originally from Hyderabad.

I build at the intersection of Apple experiences, artificial intelligence, and machine learning. My approach is practical: choose a real problem, understand it deeply, and turn it into a working product—whether that is an iOS interface in SwiftUI, an AI simulation, or a graph-based intelligence system.

Outside the code, I’m persistent, analytical, and detail-oriented. I represented Tamil Nadu in running in 2023, enjoy badminton, volleyball and football, and have spoken at the Sportstar South Sports Conclave organised by The Hindu. My long-term ambition is to become both an iOS developer and AI/ML engineer, and eventually found a technology company in artificial intelligence.
