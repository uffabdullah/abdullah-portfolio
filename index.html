I’m Abdullah Asger Ali, an 18-year-old B.Tech. Computer Science and Engineering student specialising in Data Science at VIT Vellore, originally from Hyderabad.

I build at the intersection of Apple experiences, artificial intelligence, and machine learning. My approach is practical: understand a real problem, test assumptions, and turn it into a working product—whether that is an iOS interface in SwiftUI, an AI simulation, or a graph-based intelligence system.

Outside code, I represented Tamil Nadu in running in 2023, enjoy badminton, volleyball and football, and spoke at the Sportstar South Sports Conclave organised by The Hindu. I’m working toward a career in iOS and AI/ML engineering and hope to found a technology company focused on artificial intelligence.
